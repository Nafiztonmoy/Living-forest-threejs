CSE4204   /   TOPIC 17

Find your way.

The forest changes on its own. Use the controls to move through the scene, choose a season, or change the actual scene lighting.

W / A / S / DMove the camera's viewing area
Arrow keysOrbit left/right and tilt up/down
Q / E · ShiftLower/raise camera · move faster
Mouse drag · wheelOrbit · zoom in/out
Click the forestCycle the light color
1 / 2 / 3 / 4Select Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter
SpacePause/resume seasonal progression
R · H · LReset camera · hide UI · cycle light

Manual season selection pauses the cycle; press Resume cycle to continue. Wind and water remain animated. At 1x, a full year takes 80 seconds. Performance mode switches off shadow maps.

All landscape geometry and image textures are generated in code. Foliage and river use custom GLSL shaders. A perspective camera and real scene lights complete the graphics pipeline.
